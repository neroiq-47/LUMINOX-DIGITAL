Privacy information

Pre-launch draft — not a final legal policy. Luminox Digital is a proposed brand. The company controller identity and contact details must be verified and added before public launch.

AI assistant

By default the assistant is an offline guide that runs entirely in your browser; your messages are not sent anywhere. If a live AI service is connected, messages are sent to that service to generate a response. Do not share passwords, wallet recovery phrases or sensitive personal information. Chat transcripts are not intentionally saved.

Donations

Donations are processed by Stripe on Stripe's own secure checkout page. We never see or store your card details. Stripe shares the information needed to record your contribution (such as name, email and amount) with us. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Contact form

When enabled, enquiries are delivered through a third-party form service and used only to reply to you. Until a secure delivery service is configured, the form is disabled and no enquiry is accepted.

Cookies

This website does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep operational logs. Stripe may set cookies on its own checkout page.

Your rights

Before public launch, publish verified controller contact details, lawful bases, retention periods, recipients and rights information, and a process for privacy requests.
